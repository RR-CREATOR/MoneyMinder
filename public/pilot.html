Privacy boundary

Operators see outcomes, not resident finances.

No named purchases, receipt images, household balances, or UPI IDs in partner reports. MoneyMinder never asks for a UPI PIN, holds funds, or marks a UPI claim complete until the recipient confirms it.

The next step

Start with ten complete rooms.

Fix the cohort, metrics, and kill thresholds before launch. Expand only when retained use and resident outcomes validate the program.
