Account deletion

Delete your MoneyMinder account

You can permanently delete your account from any browser without reinstalling the Android app.

  1. Sign in to MoneyMinder on the web.
  2. Open Profile from the navigation bar.
  3. Under Your data, enter your current password and select Delete account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm deletion.

Deletion removes your credentials, active sessions, server-side extension token digests, and personal receipts from the live service; uploaded image files are queued for durable deletion. A now-invalid raw token can remain in Chrome’s extension-only storage until you log out of the extension or its next rejected server request clears it. Shared ledger records remain under a non-login deleted-member identifier so balances for other household members stay correct. Encrypted database-and-image backups expire within 30 days.

If you cannot sign in, use the privacy contact published in the MoneyMinder privacy policy to request deletion.
