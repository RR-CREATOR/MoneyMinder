Pilot support

Get help with MoneyMinder

MoneyMinder is an invite-only controlled pilot. Contact the coordinator named in your pilot invitation for account access, support, privacy requests, or incident reporting.

Include

Describe the action, approximate time, device, and the on-screen request ID when one is shown. Never send your password, session cookie, extension token, UPI PIN, or real receipt image in a support message.

Self-service

  • Retry once after checking the service status with your coordinator.
  • Use account deletion for removal instructions.
  • Read the privacy notice for data and grievance information.
